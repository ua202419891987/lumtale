What big avatar apps do to non-white faces

It isn't an accident, and it isn't rare. The patterns are well documented:

  • Whitening & warping. Asian, Black, and Latino faces get lightened, narrowed, and reshaped toward a Eurocentric template.
  • Low hit rate. Even popular apps only land ~40% usable results — you pay for 50 images and keep 3–5.
  • Subscription traps. Hidden weekly charges that are hard to cancel once your card is on file.

Honest FAQ

Will my face be whitened or changed?

No. That's the entire point of LumTale. Our generation is tuned to keep your features faithfully yours.

Do you train on my photo?

No. The local preview above never leaves your device at all. For Door 2, your photo is used only to generate your portrait and is not stored or used for model training.

Is Door 1 a generator?

Door 1 is our promise page — and it includes a 100% on-device Lumling filter preview that runs entirely in your browser (no ML model download, no upload). It shows you the Lumling look on your own photo, locally. The finished, high-resolution portraits are created in Door 2 (Lumling Studio).

What if I don't want to upload at all?

Then the Door 1 preview is perfect for you — your photo is processed entirely on your device and never uploaded. You only send a photo when you decide to create a portrait in Door 2.
