Notes from the team building LumTale: why fair representation matters, where your photo actually goes, and how the Lumling style keeps your features faithfully yours.

Why this blog exists

Most AI avatar apps don't just restyle your photo — they quietly reshape non-white faces into something paler and more "standard." We started LumTale to refuse that, and this journal is where we write about it in the open.

Here you'll find honest pieces on fair representation (no whitening, no warping), plain-English explainers on privacy (what happens to your upload, and what doesn't), and a closer look at the Lumling style — our own warm, collectible-toy portrait look that keeps your identity intact.

No hype, no hidden training. Just the thinking behind portraits that look like you.
