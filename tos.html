3. Description of the Service

LumTale provides fair, identity-preserving AI portrait generation under our original "Lumling" style. We built the Service around a single promise: your face stays your face — no whitening, no warping, no training on your photo. The Service is offered through two distinct doors, each with a different privacy model:

  • Door 1 — Local Privacy Tool. Door 1 runs entirely in your browser on your own device using on-device inference (WebGPU/ONNX). The photo you use never leaves your device and is never uploaded to our servers or to any third party. This is our most private option and requires no account to explore.
  • Door 2 — Cloud Lumling Render. Door 2 produces a higher-fidelity, illustration-quality Lumling portrait. To do this, the photo you submit is transmitted to a vetted third-party GPU partner that performs the render on our behalf. We are transparent about this: before you upload, we tell you your photo is leaving your device, we link the partner's data-use policy, and we do not store or train on your images.

We may modify, suspend, or discontinue any part of the Service at any time, including adding or removing Lumling styles, adjusting quality, or changing which partner powers Door 2. We will try to give reasonable notice of material changes but are not obligated to do so for routine improvements.
